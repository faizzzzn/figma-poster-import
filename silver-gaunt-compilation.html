Chapter 01

Inspiration

Medieval armoury Reference study The starting point

The reference study. Medieval armored gauntlets — overlapping finger plates, knuckle lames, a cuffed wrist. The target: metal that moves with the hand.

Chapter 02

Figuring out the connection of joints

Sheet metal Wire · chain · pinned plates Hand-formed

Sheet-metal finger segments, cut and formed by hand. Joints tried with wire links, chain and pinned plates — version after version, fixed and learned.

Made the complete finger set

From figuring out how the frame will stay on to the finger, tried various versions, fixed, learned and made.

Chapter 03

The complete finger set

Five fingers Formed segments Wear test

Every finger built from formed segments — then worn on the hand to test whether the joints stay put and still move.

Figuring out how the joints will stay put also move was very hectic and tiring

Chapter 04

Knuckle plates & the inner glove

Knuckle lames Full hand assembly Fabric lining

Knuckle plates and the assembled hand. A black fabric glove is stitched to go inside — the hand is never exposed to metal.

Final Gauntlet made with stitching a glove inside so the hand is not exposed to metal

Chapter 05

The final gauntlet

Articulated fingers Laced cuff Finished piece

The final gauntlet. Sheet-metal cuff with cord lacing, articulated fingers, worn over the stitched inner glove.
